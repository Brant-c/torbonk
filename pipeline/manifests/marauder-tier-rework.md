# General openings and specialization finishers

Each Marauder tree retains nine one-point talents and the shared budget remains twelve. Top-row talents have no investment requirement, the middle row requires two points in earlier tiers, and the bottom row requires five. A 9/3 split still works; three-point off-spec dips cannot buy specialization finishers. Existing talent IDs are retained. Saved builds are revalidated against the new gates; invalid selections become available points rather than bypassing the gates.

| Tree | Top: general bonuses | Middle: ability upgrades | Bottom: specialization finishers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annihilation | Hungering Wounds, Saber Siphon, Ruthless Training | Deadly Focus, Lingering Wounds, Blood Rhythm | Blood Wake, Annihilation Cycle, Hemorrhage |
| Carnage | Combat Alacrity, Relentless Assault, Lasting Frenzy | Sweeping Blades, Ataru Mastery, Gore Window | Slaughter, Execute, Overwhelm |
| Rage | Empowered Blades, Execution Pursuit, Force Training | Dominate, Force Flow, Aggressive Cycle | Decimate, Rending Shockwave, Ricochet Throw |

General bonuses now work without requiring the source specialization's signature ability:

- Hungering Wounds heals one health from damaging hits, or two from bleed ticks, sharing a one-second cooldown. Its spread shield remains intact.
- Saber Siphon restores two health from learned saber hits, increasing to four against afflicted targets, once per two seconds.
- Ruthless Training replaces Deep Wounds' narrow damage bonus with 8% damage for all abilities, retaining its saved ID.
- Combat Alacrity grants 15% saber haste without activating Ataru or accelerating bleed intervals. Ataru Mastery now grants the stance in the middle row.
- Relentless Assault supports Berserk from learned saber attacks across specializations. Its drop no longer requires Carnage cleave abilities.
- Lasting Frenzy retains the general two-second Berserk extension. Frenzy recovery moves into bottom-row Overwhelm: Ravage during Gore recovers 1.5 seconds of Massacre and two seconds of Frenzy, once per activation.
- Empowered Blades grants 8% direct damage to learned saber attacks without requiring Force Crush charges.
- Execution Pursuit recovers one second of Deadly Throw and Vicious Throw cooldowns from enemy kills, including periodic kills, capped once per half-second.
- Force Training replaces Unleashed Rage's narrow bonus with 10% damage for Force Scream, Force Choke, Force Crush, Smash and Intimidating Roar, retaining its saved ID.

Matching Carnage drops inherit updated descriptions and rules. Saber Siphon's drop is no longer restricted to owning Annihilate. Duplicate suppression, relic compatibility, target limits, proc guards and original presentation remain intact. Other classes are unchanged.

Validation: build and dedicated smoke checks for all nine five-point finishers, valid three-point dips in each tree, 9/3 and 4/4/4 allocations, all 27 frame bounds, general damage bonuses, unbled saber healing, off-spec Annihilate haste without forced Ataru, saber damage without Crush, bounded execute recovery, and existing Carnage combat/drop checks. Existing Marauder combat and relic regressions also run against a temporary empty test profile, with the player's allocation restored afterward.
