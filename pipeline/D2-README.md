# SWTOR Survivors — D2

D2 extends the existing automatic ability, Health/flytext, encounter, progression and SWTOR HUD systems. Collections remains cosmetic and persistent; run items have separate `run.*` identifiers, models and ownership, with no save path.

Launch the integrated menu with `./Open-Survivors.ps1`, or go directly to Korriban with `./Open-Gameplay.ps1`. Both launchers build C#. WASD moves, Space jumps, 1–3 selects a visible choice, B returns from artifact replacement, R restarts. Hover unlocked abilities, empty action-bar slots or active artifact icons for details. Stop here for manual gameplay and presentation review.

## Configuration

- `godot/Gameplay/run-slots.tres`: four offensive slots, two utility slots, three artifact slots. `GameWorld.SlotRules` accepts another resource for future classes. Starting Force Lightning occupies an offensive slot. Unlocks are admission checked; ability replacement is outside D2. Owned upgrades remain eligible.
- `godot/Gameplay/Abilities/chain.tres`: 60 initial damage, four additional distinct-target bounces, 0.55 bounce range, 88% damage retained per link, six-second cooldown and shared GCD. Initial acquisition range is 1.1; later links may travel beyond it, with hostile/living/LOS validation from each previous link. Rendering never deals damage.
- `godot/Gameplay/Abilities/overload.tres`: utility escape: three visible hostiles within 0.32 trigger an 8-damage radial pulse and up to 0.70 displacement, on a twelve-second independent cooldown. Full-body terrain/prop sweeps and supported standing points gate each 0.015 step. Elites resist 65%; Broodmatron is immune. Surviving targets still take damage and are staggered for `StaggerSeconds` (1.5): no ordinary melee swing, and 40% movement speed scaled by knockback resistance. Boss special attacks retain their own encounter timing.
- `WaveDirector.EliteEverySpawns` / `EliteFirstWave`: every twentieth spawn from wave two is an elite variation of an existing roster enemy. No new enemy art or encounter framework. `EnemyAgent.RewardTier`: zero ordinary, one elite, two major boss. `KnockbackResistance`: 0–1.
- `godot/Gameplay/RunItems/artifacts.json`: seven unique equipment rewards plus three immediate power items. Descriptions, tiers, prerequisites, icon references and effect values are data. Major-only Codex and Conduit are excluded from elite rewards. `RunArtifacts.EliteRewardChance` (35%) gates elite rewards; bosses always pay. Elites drop nothing when no relic is eligible; power items only pad offers that contain at least one relic, or boss offers. Existing equipment is excluded; specialized items require their ability.

## Build decisions

Seven authored mechanical talents: Lightning Relay (extra chain links, two ranks), Forked Current (Force Lightning fork), Lingering Tempest (two extra Storm seconds per rank, two ranks), Clinging Storm (short slow, requires Lingering Tempest), Barrier Reprisal (depletion pulse), Dark Escape (utility-triggered speed window), and Desperate Power (damage below 35% HP). Other mechanical talents are single rank. These names/rules are Survivors designs, not claims of original talent parity. Their icons are original April ability/item art.

General stat upgrades remain available indefinitely. Offers remain three unique choices. Rarity is drawn from the original eligible catalog distribution; new choices do not add rarity mass. Within that rarity tier, unlocks, ability powers and mechanical talents have weight three versus weight one for basic stats. Existing Premium/Prototype/Artifact colors and strength multipliers are unchanged. Discrete mechanics use their documented fixed effect rather than fractional bounces or proc counts. Defensive unlocks and health remain eligible early.

Artifacts: Ancient Sith Holocron (bounce), Tomb Guardian Relic (elite-kill shield), Storm Focus (duration), Forbidden Codex (damage/health tradeoff), Ritual Catalyst (Death Field vulnerability), Dark Restoration Relic (critical-health Dark Heal), and Overcharged Conduit (every fifth qualifying lightning hit reduces another cooldown; one qualifying hit per second). Names and mechanics are authored game items, **not recovered canonical item identities**. Rarity is independent of the run-item type.

One existing enemy-death path enqueues one reward per actor identity. Selection opens on the next frame, after any current level announcement/choice/confirmation. Pending XP resumes afterward. A full loadout requires choosing one old item to replace; Back preserves the pending reward. Effects are recomputed from current equipment and trained ranks. Replacing the Guardian removes only its own active shield; replacing the Catalyst clears its vulnerability marks. Existing cooldowns and channel clocks keep their captured timing; duration changes affect the next Storm. Removing the Codex restores maximum health without healing lost HP. Restart clears equipment, pending rewards, proc clocks, ranks and unlocks.

Reward cards reuse `UpgradeCard`, B7/B10 chrome, native rarity frames, Eurostile and SWTOR button states. The single twelve-slot ability row shows only abilities unlocked this run; other slots stay empty. Empty-slot and owned-ability tooltips explain category limits. Mechanical talent cards say PASSIVE; Forked Current uses the Force Lightning icon and explicitly does not unlock the separate Chain Lightning ability. The compact artifact inspection frame is separate from the action bar. Replacement shows the selected reward's effect and each old item's lost effect.

## Source provenance and fidelity

`manifests/d2-gom-evidence.json` retains expanded April definitions and appearance schedules with bucket hashes and offsets. Chain Lightning is `abl.sith_inquisitor.skill.lightning.chain_lightning`, `0xE00085452B731E55`; its April fields include icon `chainLightning`, casting time 3, cooldown 6 and the `chain_lightning.cast_loop` appearance. Overload is `abl.sith_inquisitor.overload`, `0xE000047417B30043`, icon `overload`, cooldown 20 and 360-degree target arc. D2 timing, damage, triggers and bounce rules are authored Survivors tuning.

`manifests/d2-source.json` records exact archive paths, TOR hashes, byte offsets and decoded SHA256 receipts for the selected icons, two JBA clips and FXSpec/particle/material/texture dependencies. `d2-conversion.json` records unresampled DDS-to-PNG conversion; `d2-animation-conversion.json` records the established original rig/JBA importer and 60 Hz GLB sampling. `Assets/D2/casts.glb` is an additional animation library; locomotion, original rig/equipment and red saber remain in place.

April appearance semantics are `AbilityCastInstant2Hand1` / `AbilityChargeBack` for Chain Lightning and `AbilityAOEGroundSlam` for Overload. The bounded adapters use original `bmnnew/fp_2h_elect_instant_right_1.jba` and `fp_force_slam_1.jba`. This is a source-derived gesture selection, not a verified semantic-to-clip engine mapping. The later filename `fp_overload.jba` is absent from these April archives and was not substituted from a newer client. Both clips use the existing upper-body movement-compatible layer.

Recovered FXSpec roots: `si_chainlightning_caster_01`, `si_chainlightning_lightningcast_01`, `si_chainlightning_chaintarget_01`, `si_overload_casterhandglow`, `si_overload_casterimpacts`, all under `art/fx/fxspec/abilities/sith_inquisitor/`. Raw sockets, electrical widths/envelopes, particles and texture references are retained in `Assets/D2/source-parameters.json` and `fx.json`. Chain renders original `512_SingleArcB_bw` ribbons and electric orb impacts; Overload adapts its original electrical ring atlas into a radial wave. Engine light/zap primitives remain documented unresolved dependencies. This is a bounded Godot FX adapter, not a complete HeroEngine renderer.

`manifests/d2-audio.json` follows original FX/schedule event names through typed Wwise HIRC events, wwiser graphs and checked bank DIDX/DATA ranges to decoded unnormalized PCM. Includes Chain cast/charge/explode and Overload quickcharge/swoosh/usplode. The original mixer, random history and multilayer graph are approximated with bounded Godot voices. `scripts/prepare_d2.py`, `convert_ability_animations.py --d2` and `prepare_ability_audio.main('d2','D2',...)` reproduce the bounded conversions. No original archives or emulator files are modified.
